## Descriptions

**One line (≤ 140 chars)**
DealDock is an Instagram DM inbox for creators who sell coaching, courses and brand collaborations. Nothing sends without your approval.

**Short (about 50 words)**
DealDock is an Instagram DM inbox for creators who sell. It puts brand deals and buying enquiries first with an estimated value, drafts each reply in the creator's own voice, and sends nothing until the creator approves it. It connects through Meta's official Instagram API. Currently a small, application-only pilot.

**Long (about 120 words)**
Creators who sell coaching, courses or brand collaborations get their best leads in Instagram DMs, mixed in with fan messages, spam and requests they never see. DealDock is an inbox built for that. It sorts each conversation by type (brand deal, coaching lead, course question and more) and puts the ones likely to be worth the most first, with an estimated value. It drafts replies in the creator's own texting style, reminds them about conversations that went quiet, and records deals from agreed to paid. Nothing is ever sent automatically: every reply waits for the creator's approval. DealDock uses Meta's official Instagram API, never asks for passwords and never scrapes. It is in a small, application-only pilot.

**Never claim:** customer numbers, results, testimonials, reviews, auto-sending, bulk or cold outreach, comment-to-DM automation, scheduling, contracts, invoicing or payment collection, TikTok or WhatsApp support, or that Meta's app review is complete.
